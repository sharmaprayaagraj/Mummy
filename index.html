01

One letter, no card big enough

S

For your birthday

Dear Snigtha,

There is no card in any shop that says the right thing, so I built you a page instead. It isn't much — just light and words and a little bit of code — but every line of it is meant.

Thank you for every early morning, every packed lunch, every “text me when you get there” that you actually meant. Thank you for the patience I didn't always deserve, and for the belief you never once stopped handing out, even when I gave you no reason to keep any.

I found the photographs from that February the other day. You in the orange sari, the red suitcase in tow, a teddy bear the size of a house standing behind us. You look tired in it, and completely unstoppable. Most of my childhood looks like that from here, actually.

You turned a house into a home, and me into someone who has never had to wonder whether they're loved. That isn't a small thing. That's the whole thing.

I hope today is slow and warm, full of tea that's actually hot and people who adore you. You've spent years making everyone else's days better. Today the day belongs to you.

Happy birthday, Snigtha.
I love you more than this page can hold.

All my love, always

— for Snigtha, from your child

P.S. There's cake at the bottom of the page. I made sure.

03

Six reasons, out of a thousand

I had to stop somewhere. This felt like a fair start.

  • 01

    Your warmth

    You have a way of making a room feel like home the moment you walk into it. People soften around you. I've watched it happen my whole life.

  • 02

    Your laugh

    It arrives about a second before the joke does, and it takes everyone else with it. It's the sound this family runs on.

  • 03

    Your strength

    Quiet, steady, completely unshowy. You carry more than anyone knows, and you have never once made it everybody's problem.

  • 04

    Your kindness

    You notice the people everyone else forgets about. You always have. It's the thing I most want to learn from you.

  • 05

    Your honesty

    The advice nobody else will give me, delivered with love. Usually right. Always needed. Occasionally infuriating. Thank you.

  • 06

    Your hugs

    Empirical proof that whatever it is, it's going to be okay. Science has not yet found the flaw in this method.
